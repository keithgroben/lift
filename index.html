PROTOTYPES

Throughput games — the player is a logistics bottleneck, and the game is about seeing and widening it. The sim is pure and headless; the renderer is disposable.

Lift SimTower's real mechanic. Bottleneck: elevator throughput — car-trips per minute across the floors people need. Bloom Rush Watering plants, rebuilt on the rig. Bottleneck: you are the water delivery system — one cup, one trip down the hill, a finite day.
